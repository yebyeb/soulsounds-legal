Soulsounds

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Soulsounds (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

The short version: Nothing leaves your phone. Soulsounds has no user accounts, no servers of our own, no analytics and no advertising. Every sound file ships inside the app bundle and the music layers are generated on your device as they play, so listening works with no connection at all. The only time the App reaches a network is when you buy, restore or manage a subscription — and that is a conversation with Apple, not with us. There is nothing for us to collect, because we never receive anything.

1. What Soulsounds Does

Soulsounds plays and mixes nature soundscapes — rain, ocean, forest, crickets, and similar — so you can fall asleep, relax or focus. You can layer several sounds together, set their volumes, save the result as your own mix, and run a sleep timer that fades the audio out.

Soulsounds also offers an optional paid subscription, which unlocks layering several sounds at once and the rest of the catalogue.

All audio is bundled with the App or synthesised on your device in real time: nothing is streamed or downloaded, and playback works in airplane mode. The App reaches the network only for the subscription actions Apple handles — reading the current prices, completing a purchase, and restoring an earlier one.

2. Information We Collect

None. We do not collect, receive, or have access to any information about you or your use of the App. Specifically, the App has:

  • no account or login system;
  • no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no social-media SDK;
  • no server of our own — the App never sends anything to us, and the only network calls it makes are the App Store subscription requests described in section 6;
  • no access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

We do not build user profiles, and we do not sell or share data, because we do not receive any.

3. What Is Stored on Your Device

The App saves a small amount of data locally, using the operating system's standard app-preferences storage, so that it can remember your setup between sessions:

  • your settings — chosen language, light/dark theme, sleep-timer duration, the star sign you picked if any, the transition interval, and the name you typed if you gave one;
  • your saved mixes — the names you gave them, the sounds they contain, and the volume of each layer;
  • your playlists — their names and which of your mixes they hold;
  • the mix that was playing last, so the App can restore it;
  • how many nights you have used the App, and when the system rating prompt was last requested, so that you are not asked again too soon;
  • whether you have completed the initial mood question, whether the introductory subscription screen has been shown once, and whether a subscription has ever been active on this device — the last of these only so that the App does not show a sales screen to someone who has already paid.

This data is a few kilobytes of text. It stays in the App's private storage area on your device, it is never transmitted, and it is removed when you delete the App. We cannot read it, retrieve it, or recover it for you.

4. The Mood Check-In

The App can ask how you are feeling and suggest a matching soundscape. Your answer is used immediately, on your device, to pick a sound — it is not stored as a history, not analysed, and not sent anywhere. Closing the App discards it.

5. Background Playback

So that the sound continues when your screen locks, the App declares the iOS background audio capability and publishes the current track title to the system's lock-screen player controls. This is handled entirely by iOS on your device. No playback information is reported to us or to any third party.

6. Purchases

Soulsounds offers an optional auto-renewing subscription, sold as a monthly or a yearly plan. It is sold and processed entirely by the Apple App Store using your Apple Account. We never see or store your payment details, card numbers, or billing address, and we cannot tell who subscribed.

To know whether to unlock the paid features, the App asks the App Store on your device whether a valid subscription exists. That check is made by Apple's StoreKit framework, the answer stays on your device, and it is never sent to us. Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated sales reports that cannot identify you. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Third-Party Services

The only third party involved in normal use is Apple — for app distribution, for the subscription described above, and for the occasional rating prompt, which is Apple's own system dialog. The App embeds no other third-party service, no analytics and no advertising, and makes no network requests of its own.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, no such data can be gathered from children.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

All App data lives on your device. To remove a saved mix, delete it inside the App; to remove everything, uninstall the App. We hold no server-side copies, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no data-export request is necessary.

10. Your Rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data. Because Soulsounds does not collect or receive personal data, we hold no records about you to disclose, amend, or erase. You retain full control of your data directly on your device.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective immediately after being posted on this page, and the "Last Updated" date above will be revised. If we ever introduce an optional feature that sends data off your device, this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the feature will be off unless you turn it on.
