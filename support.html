Playback

The sound stops when I lock my screen

It should not. Soulsounds declares the iOS background-audio capability and keeps playing with the screen off. If it stops, check that iOS Low Power Mode is not aggressively suspending the app, and that Screen Time or a Focus mode is not closing it. If it still happens, email us with your iPhone model and iOS version.

I hear nothing even though the app says it is playing

Check the physical silent switch and the volume while the app is in the foreground — iOS keeps a separate volume level for media. Also make sure the audio is not being routed to a Bluetooth device in another room.

Can I use it with headphones overnight?

You can, but keep the volume low. Prolonged listening at high volume can damage hearing, and you may not hear alarms or someone calling you. Please read the safety note in the Terms of Use.

Another app's audio stopped when I started Soulsounds

That is intentional. Soulsounds takes over the audio session rather than mixing on top of whatever else is playing, because layering a podcast under rain is almost never what someone wants at bedtime.

Mixing sounds

How do I combine sounds?

Add a sound, then add another. Each one becomes a layer with its own volume slider, so you can put light rain under loud ocean, or crickets under a distant thunderstorm. There is no fixed limit — but three or four layers is usually where a mix stops getting better and starts getting muddy.

What is the difference between the nature sounds and the music?

The nature sounds are real recordings that ship inside the app. The music layers are generated on your device as they play — they are synthesised, not recorded, so they never repeat in exactly the same way and add almost nothing to the download size. They are kept in a separate group on purpose, so you always know which is which.

How do I save a mix?

Give it a name and save it; it appears under My Mixes and can be started again with one tap. Mixes are stored on your device only — see below.

The app suggested a soundscape after asking how I feel

That is the mood check-in. Your answer is used at that moment to pick a matching soundscape and is then discarded — it is not saved as a history and never leaves your phone.

Sleep timer

What does the timer do exactly?

It fades the audio down and stops playback after the period you choose, so you do not wake at 4am to ocean waves. Your last-used duration is remembered for next time.

Is the timer reliable?

It is a convenience, not an alarm. A phone call, a system update, another app grabbing the audio, or a flat battery can all change when playback actually stops. Do not depend on it for anything that matters.

Your data

Where are my mixes stored?

On your device only. There is no account and no cloud copy. If you uninstall the app or reset your phone, your saved mixes are gone and we cannot recover them.

Does the app need the internet?

No. Every sound ships inside the app and the music is generated locally. Soulsounds makes no network requests at all — it works in airplane mode.

What do you collect about me?

Nothing. No account, no server, no analytics, no advertising. Full detail: Privacy Policy.

Languages and appearance

Which languages are supported?

English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish and Japanese. The app follows your device language when it can, and you can override it in Settings.

Can I use a light theme?

Yes — Settings offers dark, light, or follow-the-system. The dark theme is the default because most people open the app in a dark room.
